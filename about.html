About Ivan

I'm Ivan, a videographer and designer who believes moments pass, and that the right image is how you keep them.

I'm based in Scotland and run ivanc.digital, a photo and video studio working with brands and businesses as a creative partner. I didn't go to art school. What I know about framing, colour and pacing came from years of shooting, editing and redoing things until they felt right.

Most of my work is short-form content made for social, alongside traditional widescreen film, including product campaigns for the clothing brand North Designerwear. Everything I make is carefully curated before I call it finished. Away from client work, I film my own travel and nature pieces.

How it started

I've loved photography and editing since school. As Instagram took off, I treated my feed like a piece of design, planning how each image sat beside the next and even posting blank squares to get the spacing right. Video followed. After a festival I came home with a lot of footage and cut it into a video that summed up the trip. I've done the same on almost every trip since: film plenty, then craft a moment out of it in the edit.

A family trip to the Philippines gave it a different weight. Everyone was together at the beach for the first time in a very long time, and with my brother's camera in hand, I became the family's photographer. I kept a minute-by-minute journal of the trip alongside the photos, from the plane landing to a single breakfast. One of the pictures was a quick shot of my grandfather, smiling.

Months later, lockdown began and he passed away. We couldn't travel to say goodbye, and there were very few recent photographs of him. That one was the picture chosen for his funeral.

It changed how I think about the work. Moments pass and don't return, and a well-made photo or film is how they are kept. That belief is behind everything I make: work that doesn't only look good but makes people feel something.
