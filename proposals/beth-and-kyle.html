Optional add-ons

Same-Day Edit

A short cinematic cut edited and screened on the day itself. £350

What it is

While your guests enjoy dinner and dancing, I'll be editing a short 3–5 minute film from the day's footage. It's then screened at the evening reception — a surprise moment where everyone gets to relive the day together, before the day is even over. It requires me to work quickly on-site, which is why it carries a premium.

You'll love this if...

  • You have an evening reception
  • You want a memorable moment for guests
  • You love surprises
  • You're big on sharing memories in the moment

Rehearsal Dinner Coverage

Coverage of the evening before the wedding. £300

What it is

A rehearsal dinner is a gathering the night before the wedding — usually an intimate meal with close family and the wedding party. It's full of candid laughter, heartfelt toasts, and calm moments that the wedding day itself doesn't always have time for. I'll capture it all and deliver it as a standalone short film, separate from your main wedding edit.

You'll love this if...

  • You're having a dinner the night before
  • You want to capture family time
  • Your wedding party is travelling in
  • You want the full story, start to finish

Social Media Cut

A vertical 60-sec edit made for Instagram Reels and TikTok. £100

What it is

A fast-paced, vertical (9:16) 60-second edit of your wedding day — built specifically for posting on Instagram Reels, TikTok, or WhatsApp. It's different from your highlights reel: shorter, snappier, and formatted for a phone screen. Think of it as the version you'd share with friends the day after.

You'll love this if...

  • You're active on Instagram or TikTok
  • You want something to post straight away
  • You want to share easily with friends & family
  • You love a quick, feel-good watch

Drone Footage

Cinematic aerial shots of your venue and surroundings. £200

What it is

Using a drone, I'll capture sweeping aerial views of your venue — the building, gardens, surrounding landscape, and the two of you from above. These shots add a sweeping, almost otherworldly quality to the final film that ground-level cameras simply can't achieve. Drone footage is woven directly into your feature film and highlights reel. Please note: flights are subject to UK CAA regulations and venue permission, which I'll confirm well in advance of your date.

You'll love this if...

  • You have a scenic or rural venue
  • Your venue has beautiful grounds
  • You want that big cinematic feel
  • You're getting married outdoors

USB Delivery

All final edits delivered on a physical branded USB drive. £40

What it is

All of your final edited films — the feature film, highlights reel, and any other add-ons — loaded onto a branded USB drive and posted to you. Your digital download link is available for 12 months, but a USB means you always have a physical backup that doesn't expire. It also makes a lovely keepsake to store alongside your wedding album and other mementos from the day.

You'll love this if...

  • You want a permanent physical copy
  • You're not great at backing up files
  • You want to watch it on your TV easily
  • You love tangible keepsakes

Good to know

Deposit & Payment

A 25% deposit secures your date. The remaining balance is due 2 weeks before the wedding.

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included. Additional rounds are available at £50 each.

Travel

Travel within 50 miles is included. Beyond that, mileage is charged at £0.45/mile. Overnight stays may apply for distant venues.

File Delivery

Finals are delivered via a private download link within 6–8 weeks. Files are hosted for 12 months — please save a local copy.

Weather & Postponement

In the event of postponement, your deposit transfers to the new date. Cancellations within 30 days of the wedding are non-refundable.

Raw Footage

Raw footage is not included by default but can be provided as an optional extra — just ask.

VAT & Contract

All pricing is inclusive of VAT. A detailed contract is provided and must be signed prior to booking confirmation.

Communication

You'll receive a planning questionnaire before the wedding to capture key moments, timings, and any special requests.
