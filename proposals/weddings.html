Full day video coverage

Your day, start to finish

  1. Hair, makeup, the nervous laughing and the quiet bits before the ceremony. I'll be there from the start, staying out of the way, so the film opens the way your morning actually feels.

  2. That first look at each other, or at the dress and the suit. It's over in a minute and you'll remember it for years. I'll be close enough to catch it, and far enough away that it still feels like just yours.

  3. I'll record the vows and readings properly, so you can hear every word when you watch it back. It's all mixed into your film and already included in your package.

  4. The toasts, the laughs, and the moment someone can't get through their words. Recorded properly again, so you won't miss a line.

  5. From your first dance right through to the evening, when everyone finally relaxes and it turns into a proper party.

Rehearsal Dinner Coverage

£300

Coverage of the evening before the wedding.

What it is

A rehearsal dinner is a gathering the night before the wedding — usually an intimate meal with close family and the wedding party. It's full of candid laughter, heartfelt toasts, and calm moments that the wedding day itself doesn't always have time for. I'll capture it all and deliver it as a standalone short film, separate from your main wedding edit.

You'll love this if...

  • You're having a dinner the night before
  • You want to capture family time
  • Your wedding party is travelling in
  • You want the full story, start to finish

Social Media Cut

£100

A vertical 60-sec edit made for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

What it is

A fast-paced, vertical (9:16) 60-second edit of your wedding day — built specifically for posting on Instagram Reels, TikTok, or WhatsApp. It's different from your highlights reel: shorter, snappier, and formatted for a phone screen. Think of it as the version you'd share with friends the day after.

You'll love this if...

  • You're active on Instagram or TikTok
  • You want something to post straight away
  • You want to share easily with friends & family
  • You love a quick, feel-good watch

USB Delivery

£40

All final edits delivered on a physical branded USB drive.

What it is

All of your final edited films — the feature film, highlights reel, and any other add-ons — loaded onto a branded USB drive and posted to you. Your digital download link is available for 12 months, but a USB means you always have a physical backup that doesn't expire. It also makes a lovely keepsake to store alongside your wedding album and other mementos from the day.

You'll love this if...

  • You want a permanent physical copy
  • You're not great at backing up files
  • You want to watch it on your TV easily
  • You love tangible keepsakes

From yes to your films

How we get there

  1. Sign the contract

    I'll send over a contract, and once that's signed your booking is confirmed.

  2. Pay the deposit

    A 25% deposit locks in your date.

  3. Planning questionnaire

    I'll send you a short questionnaire so I know the key moments, the timings and anything special you want me to catch.

  4. Pay the balance

    The rest is due 2 weeks before the wedding.

  5. Your day

    I'm there, and all you have to do is be yourselves.

  6. Your films arrive

    I'll send your finished films as a private download link within 6–8 weeks of your wedding.

  7. Revisions

    Two rounds of tweaks are included, so we can get it just right. Extra rounds are £50 each.

Travel

Travel within 100 miles of my base is included. Beyond that it's £0.45 per mile for the extra distance, return journey. If the venue is far enough that staying over makes sense, we'll agree the accommodation together beforehand.

Your files

Your link stays live for 12 months, so please save a copy to your own device.

Postponement and cancellation

If you need to postpone, your deposit simply moves to the new date. Cancellations within 30 days of the wedding are non-refundable.

Raw footage

Raw footage isn't included as standard, but I can provide it on request. Just ask and I'll send you a quote.
